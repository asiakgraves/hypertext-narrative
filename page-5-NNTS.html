NOTHING NICE TO SAY?

In her song “Nothing Nice To Say,” Jhené Aiko is having trouble 
coming to terms with the fact that she can’t defend or change a 
relationship that simply isn’t working. In the second half she 
comes to terms with the fact that she can’t save the relationship, 
and lets it go. I love this song because everyone has held onto a 
relationship (platonic, romantic, or otherwise) longer than 
they should have, and it sings about the important of letting 
things go that you tried hard to salvage.
