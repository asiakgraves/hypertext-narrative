But Why Do I Love...

DON'T BE THAT GUY?

The song Don’t Be That Guy, is about someone who’s 
relationship with Jhené has ended, and now he’s trying to win 
his way back into her life. I love this song because she chooses 
to reject him, refusing to give someone another chance after they’ve 
already hurt her. She dismisses “What could have been” because she 
knows what the outcome will already be.
