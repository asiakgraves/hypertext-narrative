I DON'T MIND?

I Don’t Mind is near the end of Jhené’s album and for good 
reason. As opposed to beginning of the album where she 
experiences sadness and self doubt, this album takes us back to 
her roots in the westside (West Los Angeles, California). She 
samples a West Coast hip-hop classic, Ice Cube’s 1993 single 
“You Know How We Do It”. I love it because it immediately makes you 
realize why she named the album Westside Whimsy. The album is a return 
to her core after feeling so disconnected.
