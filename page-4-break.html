But Why Do I Love...

BREAK?

The song Break discusses the sweet severity of experiencing 
heartbreak. It’s haunting her in her dreams, worries, and 
fears. She has no escape for it and is forced to sit in the 
feeling. I love the song so much because it could honestly 
apply to any heartbreak. A first love, a passionate hobby, 
a story you were immersed in, the list goes on. 
It’s the quintessential heartbreak song.
