But Why Do I Love...

UNICORN?

Unicorn is a song about a fantastical and idealized kind of
romance, hence it's name. I love the song because unlike her 
songs in the beginning of the album that remind you of the 
reality of love, Unicorn is meant to let you enjoy the mythical
nature of love that we often forget after being grounded in reality
for too long. It reminds me of a good romance fantasy book that
you just can't pull yourself out of!
