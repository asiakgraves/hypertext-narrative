But Why Do I Love...

NEIGHBORS (FEAT. LARRY JUNE)

I have an immense love for the song “Neighbors” because there 
is no hidden meaning or complex analysis behind it, and the 
sole purpose is to evoke the feeling of a sweet and innocent 
romance. The song even samples Mister Roger’s famous Neighborhood 
theme song, and flips the lyrics a bit. It’s a feel good romance 
about two neighbors who are feeling butterflies. It reminds everyone 
of their first school love!
