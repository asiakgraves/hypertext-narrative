But Why Do I Love...

LIKE, WHATEVER (FEAT. TYGA)?

Like, Whatever shifts into a more relaxed vibe in the album, 
and I love it because she completely omits any feelings of self 
doubt or sadness for just one moment. For just one moment, she 
stops thinking about those who don’t love her and focuses on spending 
quality time with those who do. She talks about partying, playing 
board games, and being surrounded by those who do love you.
