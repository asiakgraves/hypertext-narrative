GHOST?

I love the song Ghost by Jhené Aiko because the song talks 
about trusting and listening to yourself despite external 
distractions and doubt. By the end of the song, she loses so 
much faith in herself that she metaphorically becomes a 
‘ghost’. I can definitely relate to the overthinking and doubt 
she expresses in the song, and how it sounds as if she’s spilling 
her emotions onto the pages.
